# Editorial Home Hero Design

## Goal

Redesign the storefront home hero to follow the supplied “Natural Beauty, Every Day” reference while keeping all interface copy semantic, accessible, responsive, and compatible with the existing Soft Natural Beauty light palette.

## Approved direction

- Replace the split card with one integrated editorial composition.
- Place real HTML copy in the left 40–45% and text-free photography on the right.
- Use a high-contrast editorial serif for the headline and the existing sans-serif for interface copy.
- Keep the CTA linked to `#koleksi`.
- Present three compact store benefits with icons, without adding unsupported product claims.
- Use a text-free photographic plate derived from the supplied reference rather than shipping baked-in interface text.
- On mobile, place the artwork above the copy and keep the CTA and benefits comfortably tappable and readable.
- Preserve visible focus styles, useful alternative text, reduced-motion support, and stable image dimensions.

## Content

- Eyebrow: `Pilihan kosmetik harian`
- Heading: `Riasan simpel, cantik setiap hari.`
- Description: `Temukan pilihan makeup dengan warna natural untuk tampilan yang selalu terasa seperti dirimu.`
- CTA: `Belanja Koleksi`
- Benefits: `BPOM Resmi`, `Produk Original`, `Gratis Ongkir`

## Visual constraints

- Light mode only.
- Palette: Warm Cream `#F3EEE8`, Deep Brown `#674536`, Terracotta `#A66B55`, Soft Nude `#D6B9A7`, Charcoal `#302823`.
- No text, logos, or watermark inside the image asset.
- No hover zoom on the hero photograph.
- At 1920×1087, the hero should read as a wide editorial banner rather than two adjacent cards.
